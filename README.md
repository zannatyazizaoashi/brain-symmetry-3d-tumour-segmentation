# Learning When to Trust Brain Symmetry for 3D Tumour Segmentation

**Zannaty Aziza**

Solo undergraduate thesis for the B.Sc. in Computer Science at BRAC University,
September 2026. Supervisor: Zayed Humayun.

## Read the thesis

**[Open the thesis PDF](Zannaty_Aziza_Thesis.pdf)**

I was the sole student researcher and author under faculty supervision. My work
covered the literature review, research design, data preparation, model
implementation, experiments, evaluation, analysis and thesis writing.

The study investigates self-supervised representation learning through a
joint-embedding predictive architecture and anatomical symmetry for multimodal
3D brain tumour segmentation. It evaluates separate masking and loss components
on BraTS 2021, with regional segmentation metrics, patient-level uncertainty
analysis and failure-case investigation.
